How it works

  2. 2

    Request

  3. 3

    Receive

    Requests are completed one at a time, typically within a few business days. Ask for changes until it's right.

Questions, answered

What does "unlimited requests" mean?

Add as many requests to your queue as you like. They're worked on one at a time (two at a time on Priority), in the order you choose. Bigger requests are broken into smaller pieces so you see progress quickly.

How fast will I get my work?

Most requests are finished within a few business days. Larger builds, like a full portfolio setup, are delivered in stages so you get something useful early.

How does pausing work?

If you don't have enough work for a full month, pause your subscription and the unused days are saved for when you come back.

Do I need a particular Smartsheet plan?

No. We work with what you have. If a request would need a premium Smartsheet feature you don't have, we'll tell you up front and suggest an alternative.

How do I share access?

Share the relevant sheets or workspace with SLSC as you would with a colleague. You stay in control and can remove access at any time.

What if I just need one thing done?

Choose a fixed-price project. We'll scope it, quote it, and deliver it, with no ongoing commitment.

Is there anything you won't do?

We focus on Smartsheet and the tools that connect to it. Custom software development and work outside Smartsheet are generally out of scope, but ask and we'll point you in the right direction.
